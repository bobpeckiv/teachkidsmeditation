The approach

Mindfulness, stripped down to what works in a room full of kids.

Every lesson is written to be taught by any caring adult, read straight from the page. The language is neutral and the practices are short enough to fit between math and lunch.

Secular

No religious content and no beliefs required. Attention, breath, body, and kindness.

Short

Most practices take one to five minutes. Built for real schedules and real attention spans.

Scripted

Every meditation is printed as a read-aloud script. Nothing to stream, nothing to log into.

Adult first

Teachers practice too. Calm grown-ups are the most reliable lesson a child gets.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this religious?

No. The practices here are secular: paying attention to the breath, the body, sounds, and feelings, and practicing kindness. They are written to fit public school classrooms and families of any background or none.

Do I need meditation experience to teach it?

No. Every practice is printed as a script you can read aloud. The Aware Educator is there for adults who want to build a practice of their own first, and that tends to make the teaching easier.

What ages is this for?

Mind Muscle is written for ages 8 to 11, and most of the printables work from kindergarten through middle school. The Aware Educator is for adults.

Is mindfulness a replacement for counseling or therapy?

No. These are everyday tools for attention and calm. A child who is struggling deserves the support of a school counselor, doctor, or licensed therapist.

Can I share the PDFs with other teachers?

A personal purchase covers one classroom or one household. For a whole grade, building, or district, a site license covers everyone.
